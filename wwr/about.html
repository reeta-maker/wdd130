Exciting Expeditions

At Exciting Expeditions, our mission is to provide unforgettable rafting adventures while keeping every guest safe. We believe every river has a story waiting to be explored.

History

Growing up in Nepal, I was inspired by the country's famous rivers and exciting rafting adventures. This passion encouraged me to create exciting expeditions, where we share unforgettable rafting experience while proudly representing the adventurous spirit of Nepal.

Today we provide safe, fun, and unforgettable rafting experiences for people of all ages and skill levels while sharing our love for adventure.
